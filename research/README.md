# Research in three categories

| Category | What belongs here |
|---|---|
| [1. Pre-CMU and Hek findings](01-pre-cmu-hek/README.md) | Earlier independent leads, reliability findings and narrow nulls; later checks of those leads are labeled |
| [2. CMU and Hek reproduction](02-cmu-hek-reproduction/README.md) | Tests of the published credential channel/decoder and targeted clock/block results |
| [3. Extensions after the fact](03-extensions/README.md) | Cross-result repair bridge, receiver/mitigation questions, integrated audits and explicitly unexecuted proposals |

This is the reading order and study classification in both repos. The categories distinguish research lineage and prior knowledge; they do not infer an exact cutoff from folder dates or late commits. The first category includes follow-ups to early leads, labeled as such. “Pre” does not claim those follow-ups preceded every external report.

The periodicity study crosses categories: retrospective/targeted clock and block reproduction belongs in category 2; applying Hek's head repair to CMU-style credential dependence belongs in category 3. It is one shared frozen study, with links to the relevant sections. Reanalysis of older replies after reading the external work is reproduction-era evidence, not preexisting discovery of the 16-token clock.

[Study catalog](catalog.json) assigns every top-level `dolos-ica/experiments/` study directory, plus the early channel-decoding search and public extension backlog. Shared loaders, figures, the final report and verification tools support several categories. Source/evidence paths stay stable for frozen hashes, imports and self-contained replay. These category directories provide the human reading structure without duplicating raw artifacts.

## Research stance and judging boundary

[Research stance and competition context](../docs/RESEARCH_STANCE.md) records the supplied October 27 organizer request and its limits. The October 29 summary concerns findings made before reading the external reports. Category 1 is a research index, not an eligibility list: linked later reassessments must be kept separate, and claim-level timing needs evidence. The current public-material review does not establish a retrospective rule change; Discord and historical versions were not checked.

Attributed reproduction and extensions retain their scientific value regardless of judging scope. We preserve discovery credit, useful audits, negative results and disclosed limitations.
