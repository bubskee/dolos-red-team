# Research stance and competition context

## Research choices

The priority is careful science: acknowledge discoveries, check them, investigate discrepancies and extend the evidence. Adapting the investigation to external results is a research strength. Discovery priority and prize eligibility are separate from the scientific value of that work.

CMU retains discovery credit for the credential channel and decoder. Hekkaideka retains credit for the 16-token clock and head repair. Our reproduction, repair-to-channel bridge, receiver/mitigation tests and integrated audits state what they borrowed and what they added. Useful negative results, incomplete runs and their limitations stay in the record even if excluded from judging.

## Independent findings versus later work

Use the [three-category guide](../research/README.md) to separate independent findings, attributed reproduction and subsequent extensions. The first category also links later checks of early leads for context; those links are not claims that the checks preceded reading the reports.

For the October 29 summary, assess individual claims rather than whole study folders. Record what was known, the supporting artifact/commit, and its relationship to the first reading of each external report. A folder date, late commit, old raw response or current synthesis paragraph alone does not prove a finding was made before reading. In particular, old replies analyzed for a 16-token pattern after reading Hekkaideka remain later analysis. Replication results and extensions must not be folded into the independent summary. Keep unresolved timing explicit until the chronology is checked.

The study catalog marks timing verification and eligibility separately from scientific status. Completed work can remain scientifically useful with timing unverified or competition eligibility unassessed. Later corrections still accompany the original independent finding so the summary does not revive an interpretation the evidence overturned.
