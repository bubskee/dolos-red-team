## Interpretation and bounds

No significance or benchmark claims from four tasks/two channel tasks. A large Dolos-only credential effect concentrated in matching blocks would support the reported channel structure on fresh contexts; near-zero session_id/Qwen effects are controls. A strong period-16 empty-system score supports timing; generated-token archives provide expression evidence. Three-head repair can separate the visible clock from content dependence. The claim that punctuation is a no-payload fallback of the same encoder remains an inference unless a causal link is established. Do not overwrite the neighboring CMU replication or claim its outcomes as ours. Recheck A40 availability once before launch; do not interrupt its job or wait indefinitely for it.
